Final Conclusion

Overall results indicate that central bank communication embeddings, when integrated with macroeconomic indicators and financial market data, provide measurable predictive signal for equity market returns and implied volatility regimes, while demonstrating comparatively limited explanatory power in sovereign yield forecasting.
