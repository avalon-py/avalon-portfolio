NLP Financial Analysis Pipeline

Quantifying the impact of The Federal Reserve's Speeches on Global Assets using Multimodal Transformer Embeddings and Histogram Gradient Boosting Regressor.

Analyzing Sentiments
Recovering Alpha from 'Mere' Speech

Financial markets react not just to policy decisions, but to the nuance of communication. We built a multimodal pipeline to decode this signal.

Semantic Analysis

Ingesting 25+ years of FED speeches. Using FinBERT to extract 600-dimensional embeddings representing tone, hawkishness, and risk framing.

Macro Context

Aligning semantic signals with hard economic data from FRED. Unemployment, GDP, and Interest Rates provide the ground truth regime.

Volatility Forecasting

Modeling future returns and volatility (VIX) across SPX, Gold, and Treasuries using Gradient Boosting regression on the combined feature set.
