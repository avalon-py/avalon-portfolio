A frozen-encoder, late-fusion regressor wrapped in a retraining pipeline. Use the chapter marks on the player to jump between sections.

What it predicts

The target is the log of a video's views minus the log of the channel's trailing average: log(1+views) − log(1+trailing_avg). The trailing average is the mean of the channel's five previous uploads, so a video never sees itself. Positive means it beat the channel's usual, negative means it fell short. The prediction is converted back to an expected view count.

Only the three small branches and the head are trained. Embeddings are computed once and stored in Postgres (pgvector), so training takes minutes on a CPU.

Training setup

ChoiceValue
Loss and optimizerHuber loss, Adam, learning rate 2e-5, weight decay 1e-4
RegularizationDropout 0.2, Gaussian noise (0.02) on embeddings, early stopping with patience 10
SplitChronological 80/10/10 by publish date, so the model is tested on newer videos
ScalingScalers fitted on the training split only, to avoid leakage
DataEnglish-language personality, gaming and commentary channels, 2025 onwards, Shorts excluded
