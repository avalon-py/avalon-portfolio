Overview The bar above is the model's score: 0.33 of a possible 1.00

The thumbnail and title are the only parts of a video a creator fully controls before publishing, and they decide whether anyone clicks. This project asks a narrow question: given a thumbnail and a title, will this video do better or worse than that channel's recent videos?

I measure performance against each channel's own average of its last five uploads. Otherwise the model would just learn that big channels get more views.
